04 — the voxelbox difference

Most studios hand you a design and disappear.
We are still the ones on call at 3am.

Everything on this page runs on hardware we own, monitored by systems we wrote. That is not a flex — it is the reason nothing here has a "contact your hosting provider" step.

  • Design and code, same desk

    No handoff, no telephone game between a designer and a dev shop. The person who drew it is the person who built it.

  • AI where it earns its keep

    Models run on our own GPUs, so your data never leaves the building. We use AI where it saves you real hours and skip it where it is decoration.

  • Automation by default

    Deploys, backups, uptime probes, and alerts are written on day one — not bolted on after the first outage.

  • We run what we build

    Hosting, patching, monitoring, and the phone call when something breaks. One team owns the whole thing end to end.
