VoxelBox Studio.

A Windows desktop AI workbench for coding, research and everyday project work, and it asks before it touches your files. We're letting people in a batch at a time. Put your name down and we'll email you when your spot opens.

We only use your email to contact you about the beta. See the privacy policy. Questions? admin@voxelbox.org.

What it is

A desktop app that pairs a coding and research assistant with your own project folders.

You stay in control

It asks before it writes files or runs commands, and you can review and undo what it changed.

Platform

Windows 10 and 11.

How the beta works

Invite-only, in batches. We email you when your spot opens.

what's inside

Built to help, and to check with you first.

The beta covers the everyday work: getting code written, questions researched, and projects kept in one place.

Coding assistant

Reads your project, proposes edits, and waits for your approval before it writes anything. Changes are shown per file so you can review them.

Research

Searches the public web and reads sources for current answers, so it isn't limited to what a model remembers.

Projects that stick

Named, searchable project chats that are stored encrypted on your PC, so you can pick a project back up where you left off.

Runs on our own AI

Powered by VoxelBox's self-hosted AI infrastructure, built and tuned in-house alongside the rest of the studio's platforms.
