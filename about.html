We’re an independent digital studio. We make websites, useful systems, and original worlds — then look after what we build.

WHY WE’RE HERE

The best things
start with people.

VoxelBox began with community servers and a simple thought: a place should feel as good to be part of as it does to look at.

That thought still guides the work. A business needs a website that helps people take the next step. A game needs a world worth returning to. A tool needs to make someone’s day a little easier.

Different projects. The same care for what happens after someone arrives.
